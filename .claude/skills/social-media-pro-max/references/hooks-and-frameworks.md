# Hooks, Headlines & Copy Frameworks (cross-platform)

The hook is 80% of performance. Always draft **5–10 hook variants** and pick the sharpest. Below: a generation toolkit + proven copy frameworks.

## The 6 emotional triggers that drive sharing
People share to: **look smart, be useful, feel something, express identity, belong, or connect.** Engineer at least one. Highest-spreading emotions: awe, anger, surprise, validation ("finally someone said it"), inspiration, useful-fear ("you're doing X wrong").

## Hook generation — 20 plug-and-play templates
1. **Contrarian**: "Everyone tells you to [X]. That's terrible advice. Here's why:"
2. **Number list**: "[N] [things] that [outcome] (#[k] surprised me):"
3. **Mistake**: "[N] mistakes killing your [result] (and how to fix them):"
4. **Result + timeframe**: "[Big result] in [short time] — no [thing people assume]:"
5. **Secret/insider**: "What [authority/insiders] won't tell you about [topic]:"
6. **Before→After**: "[Year/time] ago: [low]. Today: [high]. The 1 thing that changed it:"
7. **Confession**: "I [embarrassing/vulnerable thing]. Here's what it taught me:"
8. **Curiosity gap**: "The real reason your [thing] isn't working (it's not what you think)."
9. **Steal this**: "Steal my [framework/template/system]. Took me [time] to build:"
10. **Question**: "What if [common belief] is the reason you're [stuck]?"
11. **Bold claim**: "[Strong, defensible, specific claim]."
12. **Us vs. them**: "Amateurs [do X]. Pros [do Y]."
13. **Warning**: "Stop [common action]. It's costing you [consequence]."
14. **POV/relatable**: "POV: you just realized [uncomfortable truth]."
15. **Listicle wisdom**: "[N] things I wish I knew before [milestone]:"
16. **How-to (no pain)**: "How to [outcome] without [pain]:"
17. **Story open**: "[Specific scene with stakes]. Then everything changed."
18. **Stat shock**: "[Surprising statistic]. Here's what it means for you:"
19. **Permission**: "You're allowed to [counter-cultural thing]. Here's why."
20. **Then/now reveal**: "I used to [old belief]. Now I [new belief]. The shift:"

### Hook quality test
- Specific? (numbers, names, stakes) — vague hooks die.
- Curiosity gap that only closes by reading on?
- Promises a clear, desirable payoff?
- Works standalone with zero context?
- Could *only you* (or this content) deliver the payoff?

## Copy frameworks (structure the body)
- **AIDA** — Attention → Interest → Desire → Action. General-purpose.
- **PAS** — Problem → Agitate → Solution. Best for pain-driven niches; very high converting.
- **BAB** — Before → After → Bridge. Show the gap, then the path.
- **PASTOR** — Problem, Amplify, Story, Transformation, Offer, Response. Long-form/sales.
- **4U headlines** — Useful, Urgent, Unique, Ultra-specific (hit ≥2).
- **The 4 Ps** — Promise, Picture, Proof, Push.
- **FAB** — Features → Advantages → Benefits (translate specs into "what's in it for me").
- **Hero's Journey (micro)** — ordinary world → call → struggle → win → lesson. Best storytelling shape for personal posts.
- **The Open Loop** — pose a question/tension in line 1, resolve only at the end. Drives dwell time.

## CTA library (one per post)
- Reach/share: "Repost ♻️ if this helped." / "Send to someone who needs it."
- Save: "Bookmark this for later."
- Follow: "Follow @handle for more on [topic]."
- Conversation: "What would you add?" / "Which one are you guilty of?"
- Click (where allowed): "Full breakdown in the [comments/link]."
- Choose ONE. Competing CTAs cut action rate.

## Editing pass — make any copy sharper
1. Cut the first sentence (usually warm-up). Does it start stronger?
2. One idea per post — delete tangents.
3. Replace abstractions with specifics (numbers, names, sensory detail).
4. Shorten: cut "just, very, really, in order to, I think, basically, actually".
5. Break into short lines; add white space for mobile.
6. Read aloud — fix anything you wouldn't say to a friend.
7. Strong verbs over adverbs; active voice.
8. End on a punch line, not a trail-off.

## Banned AI tells (remove before shipping)
"delve", "in today's fast-paced world", "unlock/unleash your potential", "game-changer", "navigate the landscape", "it's not just X — it's Y", "let's dive in", "embark on a journey", "in conclusion", "tapestry", "testament to", excessive em-dashes, 🚀✨🔥 on every line, perfectly balanced tricolons in every sentence.
