# Writing for Instagram — Viral Playbook

## What the algorithm rewards (2025–2026)
- **Sends/shares (DM shares)** are the #1 ranking signal for reach now. Make content people *send to a friend*.
- **Saves** (carousels especially), **watch time / completion** (Reels), **replies and comments**.
- Reels are the primary reach engine; carousels are the primary save/authority engine.
- Following count doesn't gate reach — Reels are pushed to non-followers heavily.
- Negative signals: visible TikTok watermarks, low-res, recycled stale audio, clickbait that tanks watch time.

## Reels script (the dominant format)
Length: 7–30s for max completion (shorter = higher loop rate). Story-driven can go 30–60s.

Structure:
1. **Hook (0–3s)** — verbal + visual + on-screen text simultaneously. State the payoff or the tension immediately. "Stop doing X." / "Here's how I [result] in [time]." Movement or a pattern-interrupt visual in frame 1.
2. **Retention (3–20s)** — fast cuts, no dead air, one idea, on-screen captions always. Deliver value in beats; each line earns the next.
3. **Payoff + loop (last 2–3s)** — resolve, then loop back to the start so it replays seamlessly (boosts watch-time multiplier).
4. **CTA** — soft: "Follow for more", "Save this", "Send to someone who needs it" (drives the share signal).

Reel best practices: trending-but-relevant audio, vertical 9:16, captions burned in, hook text large and high-contrast, first frame doubles as a mini-thumbnail.

## Carousel (copy side — design in `design-carousel.md`)
- Slide 1 is the hook (a thumbnail-headline). Slides 2–N: one point each. Last slide: recap + CTA ("Save this / Follow / Share").
- Caption supports the carousel: expand the story, add context, end with a question.

## Caption craft
- **First line = the hook** (only ~125 chars show before "more"). Make it stop the scroll.
- Then: story or value, short paragraphs, line breaks.
- **Call to conversation**: one question that's fun/easy to answer.
- **Hashtags**: 3–5 highly relevant, niche > broad. Place at end or first comment. Mass-tagging (#30 generic) is dead.
- Emojis: tasteful, as visual rhythm/bullets — not on every word.

## Hooks for IG
- "POV: you just realized [relatable truth]"
- "Save this before you [common need]"
- "3 things I'd never do as a [role]"
- "The reason your [thing] isn't working:"
- "Tell me you're a [niche] without telling me…"

## Voice
- Energetic, relatable, visual. Talk like a friend who's an expert.
- Aspirational + attainable. Show, don't lecture.
- Niche down — the algorithm and audience reward a clear topic identity.

## Growth system
- Reels for reach (daily if possible), carousels for saves/authority (2–3×/wk), Stories for relationship/retention (daily, polls/questions/behind-the-scenes).
- Reply to every comment in the first hour; reply to DMs (shares signal).
- Series/format consistency (same intro, same look) builds recognition and follows.
- Repurpose: one idea → Reel + carousel + Story sequence.

## Anti-patterns
- Burying the hook below the fold.
- TikTok watermark or low-res export.
- 30 generic hashtags.
- Reels with a slow 3-second intro/logo sting (kills retention).
- Engagement-bait giveaways that attract non-audience followers.
