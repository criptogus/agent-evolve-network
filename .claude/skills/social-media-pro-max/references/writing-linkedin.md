# Writing for LinkedIn — Viral Playbook

## What the algorithm rewards (2025–2026)
- **Dwell time** — how long people linger on your post (the "see more" expand is gold).
- **Meaningful comments** (longer than ~5 words) > likes. Reply to every comment in the first hour.
- **Early velocity**: engagement in the first 60–90 minutes decides reach. Post when your network is active (typically Tue–Thu, mornings local time).
- **Saves and reshares**.
- Negative signals: outbound links in the post body (put them in the first comment or use a "link in comments" note), "broetry"-spam with no substance, pods/engagement rings (increasingly penalized), generic AI text.
- Format reach order (rough, evolving): **document/carousel ≈ native text ≈ short video > single image > reshare with no comment**. Polls have cooled.

## The hook (first 2–3 lines)
Only ~140–210 characters show before "see more" on mobile. The hook must:
- Stop the scroll with tension, a bold claim, a surprising number, or a vulnerable confession.
- Create a curiosity gap that ONLY closes by clicking "see more".

Hook patterns:
- "I got fired on a Friday. By Monday it was the best thing that ever happened to me."
- "We lost a $2M client last year. Here's the email that won them back."
- "Unpopular opinion: [contrarian take your industry needs to hear]."
- "5 years in [field] taught me one thing nobody tells you:"
- "I reviewed 300 resumes last month. 90% made the same mistake."

## Body structure
- **Short paragraphs** (1–2 lines). Generous white space. This is the "broetry" layout — but keep substance high.
- One idea, told as a **story or framework**. People save and share narratives + actionable lists.
- Use concrete details: names, numbers, dates, real stakes.
- Optional formatting: a tight list with → or • ; avoid emoji bullets overload (1 type max).
- **Payoff before CTA**: deliver the insight, then ask.

## CTA / engagement prompt
- End with ONE question that's easy and inviting to answer ("What would you have done?").
- Or a soft follow ask: "I write about [topic] every week. Follow to keep these in your feed."
- "♻️ Repost if this helps your network" works when the content earns it.

## Formats
**Text post** — 900–1,500 characters is the sweet spot. Story or framework.

**Document / carousel post** — see `design-carousel.md`. Highest save rate. 6–12 slides, native PDF upload.

**Article** — long-form (1,000–2,000+ words), good for SEO/evergreen and depth, lower organic reach than posts. Use a strong cover image (see `design-linkedin-covers.md`). Promote it via a native post that summarizes the hook.

**Native video** — 30–90s, captions burned in, hook in first 3s, vertical or square.

## Voice
- Professional but human. First person. A point of view.
- Authority through specificity and lived experience, not jargon.
- Vulnerability + lesson is the most viral LinkedIn shape ("here's what I got wrong").
- Kill corporate filler: "leverage synergies", "thrilled to announce" (unless genuinely warranted), "humbled".

## Personal brand engine
- Pick **3–5 content pillars** (e.g., your craft, lessons learned, industry takes, behind-the-scenes, client wins).
- Mix post types weekly: 1 story, 1 framework/how-to, 1 contrarian take, 1 carousel.
- Comment thoughtfully on 5–10 relevant posts/day — the #1 underrated growth lever.
- Consistency: 3–5 posts/week beats sporadic bursts.

## Anti-patterns
- Links in the body (kills reach) — first comment instead.
- Wall-of-text with no line breaks.
- Engagement bait with zero value ("Comment 'YES' to get my guide" at scale).
- Humble-bragging with no lesson for the reader.
- Obvious AI tone and "I'm thrilled to share" template openers.
