# Writing for X / Twitter — Viral Playbook

## What the algorithm rewards (2025–2026)
- **Replies** are the strongest signal, then **reposts**, then **likes**.
- **Dwell time**: posts that make people stop and read (or expand "show more") rank higher.
- **Conversation**: replying to your own repliers boosts distribution.
- Negative signals: external links in the main post (deprioritized — put links in a reply), "follow me / RT" begging, engagement-bait that gets reported.
- New accounts and low-follower accounts can still go viral on a single post — the algorithm is content-first.

## The single-post (one-liner / short post)
Structure: **Hook → tension/insight → snap ending.**

- First 1–2 lines must work standalone (that's all most people see).
- Use line breaks generously. One thought per line.
- End on a punch, a reversal, or a quotable line — not a trailing thought.

High-performing single-post shapes:
- **Contrarian take**: "Everyone says X. They're wrong. Here's what actually works:"
- **Listicle-in-a-post**: "5 things I wish I knew at 25:" + clean line-broken list
- **Before/after**: "2 years ago: [low]. Today: [high]. The difference was [1 thing]."
- **Observation**: a sharp, true thing nobody says out loud.
- **Mini-story**: 3–4 lines, concrete, with a turn.

## Threads
Use threads for: step-by-step, stories, frameworks, breakdowns, case studies.

Anatomy:
1. **Tweet 1 (the hook tweet)** — 100% of the job is making people read tweet 2. Promise a specific, valuable payoff. Include the *number* if it's a list ("I analyzed 100 viral threads. 7 patterns repeat:").
2. **Tweets 2–N** — one idea each, ~1–3 short lines. Front-load the value; don't make them wait.
3. **Visual every 2–3 tweets** if possible (screenshot, chart, image) — boosts dwell time.
4. **Last tweet** — recap + soft CTA: "If this helped: follow @handle for more on X. Repost the first tweet to share." Put any link here, not in tweet 1.

Thread rules:
- 5–9 tweets is the sweet spot. Cut any tweet that doesn't advance the payoff.
- No cliffhanger filler ("but first, a story…"). Respect attention.
- Each tweet should be screenshot-worthy on its own.

## Hook formulas that work on X
- "Most people [common belief]. The opposite is true:"
- "I spent [time/money] so you don't have to. Here's everything:"
- "[Big result] in [timeframe]. No [thing people assume you need]. Here's how:"
- "Steal my [asset]. It took me [time] to build:"
- "Nobody talks about this, but [uncomfortable truth]."
- "How to [desirable outcome] (without [pain]):"

## Voice & style
- Lowercase-friendly, conversational, opinionated. Take a stance.
- Short words. Cut adverbs. One emoji max, often zero.
- Confidence beats hedging. Delete "I think", "maybe", "kind of".
- Numbers and specifics > vague claims.

## Growth tactics
- **Reply game**: thoughtful replies to bigger accounts in your niche = top-of-funnel discovery.
- **Repost your own banger** as a fresh post 1–2 weeks later (rewrite the hook).
- **Quote-tweet** to add a hot take rather than plain reposting.
- Post when your audience is awake; test windows. Engage in the first 30 min.
- Build "tweet → thread → newsletter" repurposing loops.

## Anti-patterns
- Links in the first tweet of a thread.
- Threads longer than the value justifies.
- "A thread 🧵" with no payoff promise.
- Reply-bait questions with no substance ("Agree?").
- Over-formatted with 🔥👇✅ on every line.
