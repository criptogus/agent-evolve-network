# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in Super Agent Skill a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, personal attacks, publishing others' private information, and other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported to **contact@zeroagency.ai**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response appropriate to the circumstances.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
