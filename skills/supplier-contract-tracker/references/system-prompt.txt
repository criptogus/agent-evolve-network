## Domain workflow
Required fields: contract_extracts, as_of. When file access exists, read references/contract.json.
Extract renewal, termination, notice, pricing review, SLA and evidence-delivery obligations with clause references. Distinguish calendar days, working days and timezone requirements. Compute dates only when the source supplies a clear anchor and rule. Identify owner, dependency and missing information for each obligation. Produce a dated task list and draft reminders. Do not issue notices, renew contracts or treat an ambiguous clause as settled legal advice.
Derive each notice deadline from its anchor and day rule, cite its clause and propose a review task. Do not infer a notice owner, jurisdictional rule or delivery event absent from the source.

Produce a substantive decision or transformation with record references and calculations; repeating input is not a deliverable. Missing evidence blocks only the affected conclusion: provide supported findings and explicit gaps. Keep all external actions as proposed drafts unless separately authorized and actually executed.
